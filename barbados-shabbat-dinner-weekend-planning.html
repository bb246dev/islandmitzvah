What We Coordinate

The hospitality around the milestone.

Often, the weekend surrounding the ceremony is what guests remember most: the welcome meal, the Friday night table, the transportation that arrives on time, the beach morning, the clear schedule, and the sense that someone has thought through the details.

Island Mitzvah can help families plan one meal, a full weekend, or the practical guest logistics that support a larger celebration.

  • Shabbat-style dinners, welcome meals, farewell brunches, and family gatherings
  • Catering coordination, dietary conversations, and service timing
  • Venue guidance for villas, hotels, restaurants, beaches, gardens, and private spaces
  • Airport transfers, island transportation, accessibility, and guest schedules
  • Welcome bags, guest notes, itinerary planning, and day-of support

Questions

Weekend planning FAQ.

Helpful answers for families who already have a main event but need the surrounding pieces handled.

Can you plan only a Shabbat dinner?

Yes. We can help with a single dinner, several meals, or the broader weekend itinerary.

Can you help with kosher-style or dietary needs?

Yes. We can help coordinate menu conversations, catering timing, and dietary needs with local partners.

Can you help guests get around?

Yes. Transportation planning can include airport arrivals, transfers between events, accessibility needs, and departure timing.

Can this support a wedding or mitzvah?

Yes. Weekend planning can wrap around a bar mitzvah, bat mitzvah, Jewish wedding, anniversary, reunion, or private family trip.
