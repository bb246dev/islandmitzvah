Sample Plan

A weekend that feels hosted, not hurried.

Every family is different, but a good destination simcha usually balances structure with spontaneity. Guests should know where they are and where to be but never feel rushed to get there.

Thursday

Arrivals

Airport transfers, welcome bags, sunset drinks, and an easy first dinner for early arrivals.

Friday

Island + Shabbat

Beach time, a heritage visit, room to rest, and a Shabbat-style dinner for family and friends.

Saturday

Meaning + rest

A quieter morning, ocean time, Shabbat service, Havdalah, or a relaxed family gathering depending on your rhythm.

Sunday

Main event

Ceremony, portraits, reception, music, dancing, transportation, and managing the flow of the day.

Monday

One more memory

Brunch, beach, caves, Holetown, Speightstown, souvenir shopping, or a final family outing before departures.

Tuesday

Departure details

Airport transfers, luggage timing, checkout coordination, and a smooth sendoff for guests heading home.

Planning Questions

What families need to decide early.

Our guide turns the big unknowns into a manageable first conversation. You don't need every answer before reaching out, but these are the questions that will get us on the right path.

  • Is the weekend centered on a bar or bat mitzvah, wedding, anniversary, baby naming, or family reunion?
  • Do you want a synagogue, beach, villa, garden, hotel, restaurant, or hybrid setting?
  • How much Jewish heritage, Shabbat, or Nidhe Israel context should be part of the itinerary?
  • How many guests need transportation, accommodations, meals, and accessibility support?
