Questions

Bat mitzvah planning FAQ.

A few practical answers for families considering Barbados.

Can the weekend be more than one event?

Yes. Many families plan a welcome meal, milestone celebration, beach time, heritage visit, and relaxed departure day.

Can you help with decor and creative direction?

Yes. We can help source florals, signage, tablescapes, music, photography, and island-inspired design details.

Can we include Jewish history?

Yes. Nidhe Israel Synagogue, the mikvah, cemetery, museum, and Bridgetown context can be included as part of the itinerary.

Can you help only with local details?

Yes. Some families need full planning, while others need help with vendors, venues, transportation, meals, or day-of coordination.
