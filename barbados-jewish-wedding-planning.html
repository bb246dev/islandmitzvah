Questions

Jewish wedding FAQ.

Practical answers for couples considering Barbados.

Can we have a chuppah in Barbados?

Yes. Barbados offers beach, garden, villa, hotel, and private settings that can work beautifully for a chuppah ceremony.

Can you help source a rabbi or officiant?

Yes. Island Mitzvah can help with rabbi or Jewish officiant sourcing conversations and ceremony planning context.

Can the weekend include Shabbat-style meals?

Yes. We can help coordinate welcome dinners, Shabbat-style meals, catering, and family hospitality around the wedding schedule.

Can guests experience Jewish Barbados?

Yes. Many couples include Nidhe Israel Synagogue, the mikvah, cemetery, museum, or a Bridgetown Jewish heritage visit.
